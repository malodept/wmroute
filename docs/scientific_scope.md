# Scientific scope

The generic routing rule is established adaptive-cascade/post-hoc deferral
methodology. The distinctive contribution of this release is the paired
exact-reset physical estimand, Medium-before-Full cost ledger, frozen audits,
and paper-facing reconstruction evidence. Recent world-action-model systems
already use predicted or intermediate future states to allocate computation.

## Central estimand

For a finite physical decision query, let \(L_M,L_F\) be the realized losses
of actions selected by frozen Medium and Full pipelines. With incremental Full
cost \(d_F\) and price \(\lambda\),

\[
B=L_M-L_F-\lambda d_F.
\]

Positive \(B\) means escalation improves the priced decision objective. The
router must decide from information available at its declared interface.

## What the corrected results establish

1. Medium/Full decision quality reverses across evaluated unique physical
   states and downstream tasks.
2. In PushT, the Medium-derived interface supports a frozen router that beats a
   latency-advantaged prediction-withheld task-only router.
3. In controlled PyBullet, a frozen task-prediction-regime router beats fixed,
   margin, and matched-random references after a state-pairing-corrected
   uncertainty analysis.
4. At \(\lambda=0.002\), both candidates beat standalone Medium and Full in the
   declared regret-plus-latency objective despite being slower in milliseconds.
5. PushT's advantage is limited to the low-price part of the declared grid.

## What the results do not establish

- prediction-specific value independent of state/current-latent information;
- an independent PyBullet PIV contrast;
- generic adaptive inference or cross-architecture world-model generality;
- compute saving, latency dominance, or a continuous Pareto frontier;
- Blackwell/Le Cam incomparability;
- a population PIV estimate or a finite-sample router learning guarantee;
- a Bayes interpretation of the realized-outcome clairvoyant lower bound;
- unrestricted planning, closed-loop control, or real-robot performance;
- externally certified seal chronology.

Both audits use related calibrated DINO-style interfaces. A future main-track
extension should add state/current-latent controls, a genuinely different
stochastic or recurrent world-model family, closed-loop evaluation, and
synchronized end-to-end timing.

## Prospective current-DINO/action confirmation

The prospective confirmation uses a second, non-overlapping 1,600-state PushT bank generated only after
one primary comparison, six routers, a dimension-matched current-DINO/action
projection, thresholds, latency accounting, reset seeds, and a 20,000-draw
state bootstrap were sealed. The primary effect is -0.002549 priced decision
cost with 95% interval [-0.002867, -0.002238] and one-sided 95% upper bound
-0.002286. All three fixed checkpoint-pair effects are negative. The control
routes before Medium, receives current DINO and all five actions, and pays no
DINO encoder latency. This supports incremental value beyond that tested
control, not universal causal superiority of prediction over state.
