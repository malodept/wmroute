# Manuscript claim ledger - arXiv candidate v7

## Supported headline

Under paired exact-reset PushT outcomes, a frozen Medium-derived prediction
interface supports useful post-hoc selection between separately frozen Medium
and Full predictors. On the initial fresh V106 bank it beats fixed policies,
matched routing references, and a latency-advantaged task-only control. On the
prospectively sealed V107 bank it also beats a dimension-matched,
action-conditioned current-DINO input router that is deliberately favoured in
latency accounting. Controlled PyBullet independently supports a composite
task-prediction-regime router under repaired state-clustered inference.

## V107 primary claim

On 1,600 new exact-reset PushT states, 39 fixed tasks, five candidate actions,
and three fixed checkpoint pairs at lambda = 0.002:

- prediction minus current-DINO/action priced cost: -0.002549;
- state-clustered 95% interval: [-0.002867, -0.002238];
- one-sided 95% upper bound: -0.002286;
- all three checkpoint-pair points are negative;
- the prespecified primary gate passes.

The control receives task, current DINO, and all candidate actions, has the same
65-dimensional input size, routes before Medium, and is charged no DINO encoder
latency.

## Required qualifiers

- The V107 result rejects only the tested matched current-DINO/action
  explanation; it does not establish prediction causality or universal
  superiority to current-state information.
- The routers are slower than fixed policies.
- The advantage is a regret-plus-latency result, not compute saving.
- The original V106 fixed-policy advantage is restricted to low compute prices.
- PyBullet does not independently isolate prediction from regime.
- Three checkpoint pairs are fixed-design replicates, not a population sample
  of training randomness.
- The compact package reconstructs paper-facing statistics but does not provide
  every upstream physical tensor or checkpoint required for full
  first-principles regeneration.

## Theory status

Classical/adapted: Bayes sign rule, positive-part value, conditional Jensen,
weighted sign regret, margin refinement, and upper-tail selection. Paper
specific: exact-reset paired decision losses, interface restrictions,
Medium-before-Full latency accounting, prospectively sealed controls, and
frozen audit evidence.

## Forbidden claims

- prediction is causally sufficient or universally superior to state;
- compute, energy, or latency saving;
- continuous Pareto frontier;
- confidence coverage over arbitrary training randomness;
- Blackwell or Le Cam incomparability;
- Full as a globally better expert;
- near-oracle performance;
- arbitrary-world-model, closed-loop, or real-robot generality;
- a new generic routing or value-of-information theory.
